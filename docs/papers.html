The Amazing Papers

Some questions need room. The papers take one part of the larger World Amazing framework, reconstruct the thing underneath the inherited framing, and follow the question further.

The method Do not inherit the category. Reconstruct the thing.

The framework connects the system. A paper slows one question down long enough to inspect its assumptions, causation, implications, and possible alternatives.
